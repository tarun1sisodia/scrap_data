The Hawa Mahal royal residence is actually intended to be the Palace of Winds. It is formed in a pyramid shape. This is five stories and is having 953 little windows and those are enriched with the latticework. These windows are ordinarily known as the “Jharokhas” which is developed so that it would appear that a honeycomb. Maharaja Sawai Pratap Singh ordered the development of the Hawa Mahal in 1799. Essentially, it is built inside the City Palace and expands the Zenana or ladies’ chambers. The upper floors of the royal residence were built for regal ladies to watch regular day to day existence in the bazaar road beneath.
