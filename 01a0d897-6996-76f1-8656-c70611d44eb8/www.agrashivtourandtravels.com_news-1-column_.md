Despite the oppressive heat that pervades most of India, there are still several locations that are ideal for a summer holiday. A weekend break with friends or a week-long solo excursion are the greatest ways to enjoy summer in the United States. However, deciding on the ideal location for your vacation might be a challenging task. As a result, we’ve compiled a list of places in India to visit in the summer. ## Manali

Manali’s breathtaking snow-capped mountains are enhanced by the river’s glistening waters. The breathtaking Rohtang Pass, the Nyingma PA Buddhist Temple, and the Himalayan National Park are just a few of the awe-inspiring attractions in the area. You may participate in rafting, hiking, and camping, among other activities. Visit the [amazing place](https://www.agrashivtourandtravels.com/best-places-to-visit-in-manali-hire-outstation-taxi/) in india by booking the best travel packages in India.

## Kashmir

Kashmir may have been the first summer destination we came upon. The holy trinity of Gulmarg, Srinagar, and Pahalgam will meet all of your tourist needs. Relax on a houseboat trip or enjoy a Shikara ride on the lake’s glittering water. The valleys of Srinagar are dotted with blossoming tulips.

## Shimla

Located in the Himalayas, Shimla is a beautiful blend of contemporary India and colonial heritage. The Viceregal Lodge and the bustling Mall are also worth a visit for a taste of the local flavour. Visit Shimla to discover its rich history and natural splendour.

## Darjeeling

A well-known brand that comes to mind whenever someone mentions taking a summer vacation! This little mountain town, perched high in the majestic Himalayas, has a lot to offer visitors. Taking a stroll along the twisting lanes, you can smell the ethereal aroma of tea at every turn. You may eat at Glenary’s, ride the Toy Train, or shop in Chowrasta, all within a few blocks of each other.

## Gangtok

With its vibrant urban life and tranquil hill station life, Gangtok is the ideal place for a relaxing holiday. The Rumtek Monastery, the Teesta River, and the Nathu La Pass are all great places to visit if you’re want to get a taste of the area’s spirituality. If you’re in the area, don’t miss out on the local food.

## Mussoorie

The settlement is at a height of 7000 feet, surrounded by mountains. This is the explanation for the unusually cold temperatures, which give a welcome break from the oppressive heat. Boating on Mussoorie Lake, shopping at the Library Bazaar, and hiking to the Cloud’s End are just few of the activities available in this area.

## Nainital

This hill station in Uttarakhand is one of the best summer holiday destinations in India. It is replete with several lakes that add to its old-town charm. Surrounded by rolling hills, it provides for a quick escape for people, especially in Delhi and Chandigarh. Some of the popular tourist spots are Nainital Lake, the Naina Peak, and the High-Altitude Zoo. You can also experience activities like boating and paragliding.

## Ooty

In the southern part of the nation, this little village is a great place to spend a summer vacation. It has been a famous tourist attraction since the days of British rule, and with good reason. Ooty is a must-visit for nature lovers. Ride the historic Nilgiri Railways, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, relax in a boat on the serene Ooty Lake, or watch the sun set over the Ooty Lake from the vantage point of the nearby Doddabetta Mountain.

## Andaman and Nicobar Islands

With so many islands to choose from, it’s easy to become overwhelmed by the sheer number of fun things to do. In addition to these, there are several more islands in the area such as Havelock Island, Ross Island, and more. Snorkeling, scuba-diving, and sea-walking are just few of the activities available at this location.

## Shillong

You’ve now learned that India’s Northeast is home to some of the country’s top summer vacation spots. Beautiful lakes and cascading waterfalls abound in Shillong, which is known as the “Scotland of the East.” The Shillong Peak, the Elephant Falls, and the Living Root bridge are just few of the attractions in the area. Trekking, camping, and even waterfall rappelling is just a few of the exciting activities available to you.

## Mahabaleshwar

Mahabaleshwar, a hill station in Maharashtra, is a popular destination for Mumbai residents. One of India’s few evergreen forests may be found in the area, making it famous for its beautiful foliage. Do not miss the Elephant’s Head Point, Venna Lake, and Lingamala Falls while you are here.

## Mysore

You won’t be able to choose a favourite among Mysore’s several beautiful palaces, which are all stunning in their own right. The Mysore Palace, the Brindavan Gardens, the Sri Chamundeshwari Temple, and many more popular tourist attractions can be found in Mysore. Mysore is a great place to spend your summer vacation if you’re want to learn about Indian culture and beliefs.

## Kasol

Yet another gem of Himachal Pradesh, Kasol is a small town that is increasing in its popularity, especially amongst trekkers, backpackers, and nature lovers. Here, you can visit the Gurudwara at Manikaran Sahib, jump across the small boulders on the Parvati River, and revel in the utopic Tosh village.

## Auli

A summer vacation in Auli is the perfect getaway. Known for its skiing on the snow-covered slopes of the Alps, it’s a popular destination. The awe-inspiring Himalayan background is complemented with apple orchards and pine forests. There’s also the Auli ropeway, which will carry you 4 kilometres into the countryside and give you a great perspective of the lush pastures and meadows below.

## Dharamshala

Dharamshala, in addition to its natural splendour, is spiritually important as the seat of the Dalai Lama. Its architecture and culture are also heavily influenced by Tibetan elements. The Dharamshala Cricket Stadium is the place to be if you’re a cricket lover like the rest of us. Trekking, camping, and paragliding are just a few of the many adventure activities available.

## Pondicherry

As the “French Capital of India,” Delhi is full with reminders of France’s rich history and culture. The beaches and cafés that line them round out the picture of an ideal Indian summer holiday. The Rock beach, Auroville, and the opulent French cuisine and delicacies are just a few of the options available to visitors.

## **Places To Visit In Himachal Pradesh**

There are so many popular destinations like Kullu-Manali, Shimla, Dalhousie and Dharamshala, do go on an offbeat vacation by picking one of these places Bir Billing, Malana, Khajjiar, Mashobra, Kinnaur, Chail & McLeod Ganj. You can explore these places and enjoy your journey to the Himachal.

### Kasol

Kasol is located on the banks of the Parvati River. It lies a photogenic hamlet of Kasol, also referred to as the ‘Little Israel of India’. Kasol is popular among backpackers and hippies. You can enjoy the stunning landscape of snow-capped mountains, lush valleys, scenic waterfalls and undisturbed trekking routes. It makes it a paradise for nature lovers and trekkers.

### Shimla

Shimla is a gorgeous colonial hill station, that is known as Queen of Hills. This is a perfect place to wander. Shimla showcases the best colonial-era architecture such as the Viceregal Lodge, the Town Hall, Gaiety Theatre and the Christ Church. Shimla is located at the foothills of the Himalayas amidst the lush greenery and snow-covered mountains make it a perfect place for trekking and other outdoor fun.

### Kasauli

Kasauli is located at an elevation of 1,900 meters. Its beauty and appeal lie in its unadulterated air, serene and peaceful vibe, and an abundance of nature is what attracts travellers. You can visit places such as Baptish Church, Christ Church, Kasauli Brewery, Monkey Point, Nahri temple and Kasauli Club.

### Dharamshala

Dharamshala is located in the upper reaches of Kangra Valley. You can enjoy one of the best climates in the Himachal. The place is surrounded by snow-laden peaks of Dhauladhar mountains and lush pine and deodar forests. It is brimming with cultural and architectural attractions, along with a great number of restaurants, cafes and shops catering to its extensive multicultural Indian and Tibetan communities. You can explore McLeod Ganj (India’s Mini Tibet), Dharamkot, Sindhbari, Ramnagar and Naddi.

### Manali

Manali is situated at an altitude of 2,050 meters on the River Beas valley. It comprises lush pine and deodar forests, snow-covered mountains, scenic meadows, waterfalls and valleys that attract adventurers and nature lovers alike. You can also explore the ancient temples and Tibetan monasteries beckon spiritual enthusiasts.

### Dalhousie

Dalhousie will make you fall in love with mountains, cascading waterfalls, lakes and lush pine and oak trees. People love to come to Dalhousie to partake in outdoor activities, like trekking, river rafting, canoeing, kayaking and camping. While the peaceful atmosphere and pristine nature lure nature lovers and peace seekers alike.

## **Temples**

Himachal Pradesh is known as the Land of the Gods. Each and every temple has its own deity and a rich mythological past. These temples attract a lot of devotees from across the world. It also contributes to tourism. You can try and visit these popular places such as Sankat Mochan Temple, Tara Devi Temple, Hidimba Temple, Nahar Singh Dhaulra, Vyas Gufa & Bhootnath Temple.

### Wildlife

Himachal Pradesh offers a thrilling wildlife experience that is unparalleled, simply because of the presence of rare wild animals that are indigenous to this region. It has numerous national parks and sanctuaries where you can get a glimpse of these untamed creatures. You can either go on a trek, book a spot on a safari or even camp out in the open to truly enjoy the state’s wildlife. There are a lot of sanctuaries such a Kalatop-Khajjiar Sanctuary, Daranghati Sanctuary, Pin Valley National Park

Chail Sanctuary, Maharana Pratap Sagar Sanctuary, Great Himalayan National Park and Shri Naina Devi Sanctuary.
